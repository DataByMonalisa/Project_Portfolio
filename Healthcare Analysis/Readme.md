# ✅ 📑 Healthcare Dashboard — Final Documentation
## 1️⃣ Purpose
To provide clear, connected insights across Patients, Doctors, Appointments, Treatments, and Revenue — for improved decision-making in a healthcare setup.

## 2️⃣ Dashboards Created
### A) Patient Dashboard

- Key Focus: Individual patient’s journey — demographics, appointment history, treatments received, billing status, insurance info.

- Main Visuals:

* Patient Profile (Name, Age, Gender)

* Appointment History table with status highlights

* History table with type, cost, date

* Billing Status Donut (Paid vs Pending)

* Payment Details table

* Insurance provider & number

* Cards for Total Billed, Total Paid, Total Pending

#### ✅ Key Insight: One click shows a complete patient financial & medical profile.

### B) Doctor Dashboard

- Key Focus: Individual doctor’s workload, revenue contribution, and specialties.

- Main Visuals:

* Doctor Profile Card (Name, Specialization, Branch, Years Experience)

* Total Appointments handled (Card)

* Unique Patients seen (Card)

* Revenue generated by the doctor (Card)

* Appointment Status Donut (Scheduled, No-show, Cancelled)

* Revenue Trend Over Time (Line)

* Treatments performed (Bar Chart)

* Revenue by Branch (Treemap or Bar)

#### ✅ Key Insight: Easily spot top-performing doctors & specializations, monitor no-show impact on revenue.

### C) Appointment Dashboard

- Key Focus: Overall operational snapshot of all appointments.

- Main Visuals:

* Total Appointments (Card)

* Appointment Status Breakdown (Donut)

* Appointments Over Time (Line)

* Appointments by Doctor (Bar)

* Appointments by Specialization (Bar)

* Common Reasons for Visits (Column)

* Appointments by Time Slot (Column)

* No-show % (Card)

#### ✅ Key Insight: Identify booking trends, peak hours, and areas to reduce no-shows & cancellations.

### D) Revenue Dashboard

- Key Focus: Complete financial overview for management.

- Main Visuals:

* Total Revenue (Card)

* Paid vs Pending Revenue (Donut)

* Revenue Trend Over Time (Line)

* Revenue by Payment Method (Donut/Bar)

* Revenue by Doctor (Bar)

* Revenue by Branch (Treemap or Bar)

* Revenue by Treatment Type (Column)

#### ✅ Key Insight: Understand cash flow, payment method mix, top revenue drivers, and outstanding dues.

## 3️⃣ Key Measures & DAX
- <b> Total Revenue: </b> SUM(Billing[amount])

- <b> Total Paid Revenue: </b> CALCULATE(SUM(Billing[amount]), Billing[payment_status] = "Paid")

- <b> Total Pending Revenue: </b> CALCULATE(SUM(Billing[amount]), Billing[payment_status] = "Pending")

- <b> Total Appointments: </b> COUNT(Appointment[appointment_id])

- <b> No-show %: </b> DIVIDE(COUNTROWS(FILTER(Appointment, Appointment[status] = "No-show")), [Total Appointments])

- <b> Unique Patients: </b> DISTINCTCOUNT(Appointment[patient_id])

- <b> Doctor Revenue: </b> CALCULATE(SUM(Billing[amount]), TREATMENT[appointment_id] IN VALUES(Appointment[appointment_id]))

## 4️⃣ Key Relationships
** Billing ⟶ Treatment ⟶ Appointment ⟶ Doctors + Patients

** Ensures all visuals respond correctly to filters like doctor name, patient name, date range.

## 5️⃣ Key Takeaways
#### ✅ Clear 360° view of patient financials
#### ✅ Revenue leakages can be caught early (pending vs paid)
#### ✅ No-show trends help tighten scheduling & reminders
#### ✅ High-performing doctors & branches stand out
#### ✅ Treatment-wise revenue helps guide resource allocation

## 6️⃣ Recommendations
#### ✅ Monitor the pending vs paid ratio monthly — automate reminders for overdue bills.
#### ✅ Analyze no-shows — send SMS/email reminders to patients with high no-show rates.
#### ✅ Use treatment revenue split to decide where to invest in equipment & staff training.
#### ✅ Reward doctors with high patient satisfaction & revenue contribution.
#### ✅ Regularly update insurance info to avoid claim rejections.
